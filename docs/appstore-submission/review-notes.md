## English (paste this)

Carelogue Plus — $3.99/month with a 1-week free trial — is the app's only subscription. The app has no account system and no sign-in of any kind (the device's iCloud account is used only for the user's own syncing and sharing).

All record-keeping features (journeys, logs, attachments, visit recordings) are free; the subscription unlocks AI features only.

How to test:
1. Open the app, create or open a Journey, open a medical report, and tap "Explain".
2. The paywall appears — subscribe with your sandbox Apple ID to unlock.
3. AI features require the subscription and a network connection.

Journey sharing (new in 1.0) uses Apple's CloudKit sharing: tap the person icon at the top right of a journey timeline to invite someone through your own iCloud. Testing it end-to-end needs a second Apple ID; everything else in the app works without sharing.

About the AI: explanations are generated by a third-party large language model (DeepSeek, hosted by DeepInfra). Only text from the user's own records is sent for processing; no health data is stored on our servers. The app provides no diagnoses or medical advice.

The subscription supports Family Sharing.

## 中文对照

Carelogue Plus（$3.99/月、首周免费）是唯一订阅；app 无账号、无任何登录（设备的 iCloud 账号只用于用户自己的同步与共享）。
所有记录功能（旅程、日志、附件、面诊录音）免费；订阅只解锁 AI 功能。
测试路径：打开 app → 进入任一旅程 → 打开一份报告 → 点「解释」→ 出现订阅页 → 用沙盒 Apple ID 订阅。
旅程共享（1.0 新增）走 Apple 的 CloudKit 共享：旅程时间线右上角的人形图标，通过用户自己的 iCloud 发邀请。完整测试需要第二个 Apple ID；不共享也不影响 app 其他功能。
AI 说明：第三方大模型（DeepSeek，DeepInfra 托管）生成解释；只发送用户自己记录的文字，服务器不留存；不提供诊断或医疗建议。
订阅支持家庭共享。

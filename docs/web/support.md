## 中文

### 关于 Carelogue

Carelogue 是一个记录就医过程的 iPhone 应用：就诊、检查、测量和随手记放进一条
时间线；对着报告拍一张，用大白话讲给你听；面诊录下来，转写并整理成总结。它是
记录工具，不提供诊断或医疗建议——健康问题请始终咨询你的医生。

### 常见问题

**订阅怎么取消？**

订阅由 Apple 处理：iPhone「设置」› 顶部你的姓名 ›「订阅」，或 App Store 账户
页 ›「订阅」，选择 Carelogue Plus 取消。取消后当前计费周期内继续可用。

**怎么申请退款？**

App Store 的购买与退款由 Apple 处理，我们无法直接操作。请在
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) 用购买时的 Apple ID
登录并提交申请。

**我的数据存在哪里？**

记录、附件与录音保存在你的设备和你自己的 iCloud 私有库里。Carelogue 没有账号
系统，我们的服务器上没有你的健康数据。详见
[隐私政策](https://carelogue.ca/privacy)。

**怎么删除数据？**

「设置」›「数据管理」›「清空所有数据」会删除这台设备上的全部记录、附件与档
案；删除 App 也会一并删除本机数据（iCloud 中的副本按 Apple 的规则清理）。

**共享给家人不工作？**

共享以「旅程」为单位，通过你们各自的 iCloud 完成：双方都需要登录 iCloud，对方
需要接受邀请。入口在时间线右上角的共享图标；任何一方都可以随时停止共享，停止
后对方设备上已有的副本会保留，但不再更新。

**AI 解释没有结果？**

报告解释、面诊总结与疑问翻译需要 Carelogue Plus 订阅（首周免费试用）。请确认
订阅有效、设备联网、报告照片清晰。为防止滥用，AI 功能每小时有使用上限，稍后再
试通常即可。录音的转写在本机完成，不需要订阅、不消耗额度。

## English

### About Carelogue

Carelogue is an iPhone app for keeping your care records: visits, test results,
measurements and everyday notes on one timeline; a photo of a report, explained
in plain language; an appointment recorded and summarised. It is a
record-keeping tool, not a source of diagnoses or medical advice — always talk
to your doctor about your health.

### Frequently asked questions

**How do I cancel the subscription?**

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iPhone Settings › your name at the top ›
Subscriptions, or your App Store account page › Subscriptions, then cancel
Carelogue Plus. It keeps working until the end of the current billing period.

**How do I request a refund?**

Purchases and refunds go through Apple; we cannot issue them directly. Sign in
with the Apple ID used for the purchase at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and submit a
request.

**Where does my data live?**

Records, attachments and recordings stay on your device and in your own private
iCloud. Carelogue has no accounts, and there is no copy of your health data on
our servers. See the [privacy policy](https://carelogue.ca/privacy).

**How do I delete my data?**

Settings › Data Management › Erase all data removes every record, attachment
and profile item from this device. Deleting the app also removes its local
data (the iCloud copy is cleaned up under Apple's rules).

**Sharing with family doesn't work?**

Sharing is per journey and runs through each of your own iClouds: both of you
need to be signed in to iCloud, and the invited person has to accept the
invitation. The entry is the share icon at the top right of a timeline; either
side can stop at any time. The copy already on the other device stays there
but stops updating.

**An AI feature returned nothing?**

Report explanations, visit summaries and question translations need a Carelogue
Plus subscription (one-week free trial). Check that the subscription is active,
the device is online, and the photo of the report is clear. To prevent abuse,
AI features have an hourly limit — trying again a little later usually works.
Transcription of recordings happens on your device; it needs no subscription
and uses none of the quota.

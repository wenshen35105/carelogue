# 1.0.1 · What's New in This Version

> 用途：ASC 版本页「What's New in This Version」字段——**English (Canada)** 与**简体中文**两个本地化各贴对应版本。
> 来源：`docs/releases/1.0.1/m7-tasks.md`（T42–T50）。用户视角，不用任务编号。

## English (Canada)

```
What's new in this version

• Visits now record a specific time, not just a date — your timeline shows when each appointment happened.
• Measurements can carry a note.
• Location and doctor fields suggest what you've entered before, so you don't retype the same hospital or clinician.
• Recording no longer keeps you on one screen: browse your journey, check your questions, or lock your phone — the recording continues, with a small recorder bar to return to.
• Visit transcription now works out whether the conversation is in English or Chinese and transcribes in the right language — mixed-language visits come out far more readable. You can also re-transcribe an older recording from its ⋯ menu, and share a recording together with its transcript.
• Recording playback now has a seek bar — jump to any moment in a long visit.
• Journeys can now be deleted, with a clear confirmation that lists everything that will be removed.
• Fixed an issue where a shared journey could occasionally lose a transcript or an explanation during sync.

Thanks for using Carelogue. Questions or feedback: support@carelogue.ca
```

## 简体中文

```
1.0.1 更新内容

• 就诊记录现在能记具体时间，不只日期——时间线上一目了然。
• 测量记录可以加备注了。
• 填写地点、医生时自动联想以前输过的内容，不用反复重打。
• 录音时可以离开录音页：继续浏览旅程、翻看疑问，甚至锁屏录音也不会断；底部有小录音条随时回去。
• 面诊转写会自动判断说的是中文还是英文，用正确的语言转写——中英混说的面诊，转写可读性大幅提升。老录音可以在「⋯」菜单里重新转写，也能把录音和转写原文一起分享出去。
• 录音回放支持拖动进度，长录音不用干等。
• 旅程可以删除了，删除前有明确确认，列清会被删掉的内容。
• 修复：共享旅程偶尔会在同步时丢转写或解释的问题。

感谢使用 Carelogue。问题或建议：support@carelogue.ca
```

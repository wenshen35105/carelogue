# 1.1 · What's New in This Version

> 用途：ASC 版本页「What's New in This Version」——English (Canada) 与简体中文各贴对应版本。
> 来源：M8 T51–T61（求好评为用户无感项，不入更新日志）。

## English (Canada)

```
What's new in this version

• Medications, now built in: keep a list of what you take, with reminders at the times you choose — and link each one to a journey.
• Reminders for upcoming visits, with custom times set per appointment.
• Apple Health, connected: bring your weight, blood pressure, heart rate and temperature readings into a journey, and write measurements you record back to Health (you can switch this off).
• A new journey header: pin the details that matter (due date, blood type, doctor) as chips, and follow a progress ring toward a target date.
• Blood pressure now records both numbers — shown as 120/80, with both lines in the trends chart.
• A Home Screen widget: your next appointment at a glance, plus a quick-note shortcut.
• Quick notes can now hold photos and files, like visits and reports.
• Places now suggest real hospitals and clinics as you type (via Apple Maps).
• A journey can be marked as recording someone else's care — handy when you keep records for a family member. Recordings and Apple Health stay with the right person.

Thanks for using Carelogue. Questions or feedback: support@carelogue.ca
```

## 简体中文

```
1.1 更新内容

• 用药管理：记录你在服的药，按你选的时间点提醒，还能关联到旅程。
• 复诊提醒：每段就诊可以单独设置提醒时间。
• 接入 Apple 健康：把体重、血压、心率、体温读数带进旅程；在 Carelogue 记的测量也可以写回「健康」（可关闭）。
• 全新的旅程头部：把重要信息（预产期、血型、医生）固定成标签，显示目标日期的进度环。
• 血压支持双值记录——显示为 120/80，趋势图里两条线。
• 主屏小组件：一眼看到下次就诊，还能快速记一笔。
• 随手记也可以添加照片和文件了。
• 输入地点时自动联想真实的医院和诊所（来自 Apple 地图）。
• 旅程可以标记为「为家人记录」——帮家人管理就医记录时，录音和 Apple 健康数据会留在正确的人名下。

感谢使用 Carelogue。问题或建议：support@carelogue.ca
```
